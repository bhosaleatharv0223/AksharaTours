Our Policies

We believe clear information makes every journey easier. Please take a moment to review our policies.

Fees once paid are non-refundable, except for duplicate payment or payment made in error, which is refunded to the original payment method within 5 to 7 working days. To cancel the service, parents must inform us in writing before the start of the next month.

Fees must be paid monthly by the due date. Reminders are sent on WhatsApp for unpaid fees. Service may be paused if fees remain unpaid after reminders. Fees may change with prior notice to parents.

We collect only the student name, parent name and phone number needed to run the service and send fee reminders and payment confirmations. We do not sell or share personal data, except with payment and messaging partners required to process payments and send messages. To correct or delete your data, contact us by email.
